Sumas (you) received a distress signal from the planet Unicon-6 and landed to investigate. Meeting Neera, Sumas learns that the Blerguns have locked away the unicorns. With unicorns sealed, their rainbows can't bathe the world in color making it dull and bland. Free the unicorns and beat back the Blerguns to save the planet!

* Movement: wasd or arrow keys
* Jump: w or up
* Shoot: space
